# Threat of Climate Change on O'ahu Waterfront Development
## Tatar Anurakwongsri

Island nations are at the most risk under climate change projections. Many islands will be submerged
in the future at the current rate of global warming, with effects already being seen around the globe.
Hawaii is one the places that may experience disproportionate effects from climate change. There are many
climate scenarios, so it is important that we look different scenarios as well. 

The target audience for this project are policy developers who are interested in seeing the effects of climate
change on a  geospatial level. This story map explores population and development on the island of O'ahu, looking at past data and present policies to better understand what is in store for the future.
